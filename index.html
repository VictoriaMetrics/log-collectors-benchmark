Methodology

Each collector runs on its own, with no other collectors on the Node. All log generator Pods produce the same kind of JSON records, see log format for details.

Every collector is limited to 1 CPU and 1 GiB of memory. Configs are based on the official Helm chart values and may include tuning to improve collector performance. The collector names in the tables link to the exact configs of each run.

Throughput is counted at the receiving end, not by the collectors themselves. A collector is marked as failed when its container restarts or when logs are lost. Its numbers still show what it reached before that.

The benchmark runs on a c2d-highcpu-8 virtual machine with a local SSD. Apart from the log generator and the collector under test, the Node only runs the benchmark components: log-verifier, VictoriaMetrics and Grafana.

Kubernetes log rotation settings are kept at their defaults: maximum log file size is 10 MiB, with checks running every 10 seconds.

Send an updated collector config if you know how to speed it up by more than 10% without introducing unusual production settings.
